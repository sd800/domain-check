Your interest.
Your business.你的关注，

It shouldn’t become a signal to a commercial search site.查询域名，是为了获取信息。

The registry注册局

Registry first. Registry only. Queries go to registry services. Registrar referrals are never followed.查询到注册局为止。直接使用注册局服务，不跟随任何注册商转介。

Processed on your Mac. No accounts, no analytics, and no lookup history kept by Domain Check.在本机处理。无需账号，没有分析追踪，Domain Check 不保留查询记录。

installer=$(mktemp)

curl -q --proto '=https' --proto-redir '=https' --connect-timeout 15 \
  --max-time 180 --max-filesize 1048576 -fsSL --retry 3 -o "$installer" \
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sd800/domain-check/main/scripts/install.sh
sh "$installer"
